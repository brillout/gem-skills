# Ideas

## TLDR improvement ideas

- **Compare with neighbours.** `grill-me` also interviews you, but ends in a plan; this one ends in raw writing material. One “unlike X” clause can do a lot.
- **Include practical facts that change the decision**, such as “only runs when you invoke it” (from disable-model-invocation) or “writes a file”. Tags cover some of this, but a sentence is easier to read.
- ****Test with a cold reader.** After writing a TLDR, give it to a fresh agent with no other context and ask what the skill does. Compare the answer with the SKILL.md. If they don’t match, rewrite. This catches exactly the failure above.
